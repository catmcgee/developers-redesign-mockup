Charge a few cents per API call

On internet capital markets anything can have a price, down to a single request. Price each request at a cent or less. Clients, whether people's apps or AI agents, pay inline over HTTP, with no signup, API key or invoice.

✓ A good fit if

  • You want to price per request, per page or per second of compute
  • Your clients are programs or AI agents, not people at a checkout
  • You don't want to run signups, keys and balances

✕ Not the right fit if

  • You charge one flat monthly fee; use your existing billing
  • Your buyers need invoices and net-30 terms
  • Requests must answer in single-digit milliseconds even on the first call; use prepaid sessions instead
FeesNew conceptLearn more →
Every transaction pays a small flat network fee, currently a fraction of a cent, whatever the amount. Whoever submits the transaction pays, and that can be you rather than your customer.
Like:A fixed per-API-call cost, not a percentage interchange fee.

Design notes

Why this page looks the way it does. These are for reviewers, not part of the product.

WhyThis path covers what today's nav calls Agents and AI, so it doesn't need its own Wave 1 path. Pay-per-call is the concrete job agents do with Solana.
Vs. todayToday this content is split across Payments → Agentic payments → x402, pay.sh and Resources → x402 facilitator. Here it becomes one use case with its own tab.
WhyThe fit check is honest about latency. The first paid request includes a settlement round-trip, so it's not for sub-10 ms SLAs without sessions.

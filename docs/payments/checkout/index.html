Is this the right tool?

Most teams compare this with cards, so here is that comparison, for a $20 sale:

Cards (e.g. Stripe)Bank transferUSDC on Solana
Fee~$0.88 (2.9% + 30¢)$0–$30~$0.001
Money usable by you~2 business days1–5 days< 1 second
ChargebacksYesRareNo. Refunds are a payment you send back.
Works across bordersWith FX feesSlow, costlySame as domestic
Your customer needsA cardA bank accountA wallet holding USDC

✓ A good fit if

  • You sell across borders, or to people without cards
  • Your margins or ticket sizes are small
  • You want instant payouts or automatic revenue splits

✕ Not the right fit if

  • Your customers won't use a wallet and you won't onboard them. Keep cards, or add an on-ramp
  • You need card-style buyer protection
  • You haven't checked how your jurisdiction treats stablecoins
FeesNew conceptLearn more →
Every transaction pays a small flat network fee, currently a fraction of a cent, whatever the amount. Whoever submits the transaction pays, and that can be you rather than your customer.
Like:A fixed per-API-call cost, not a percentage interchange fee.

Design notes

Why this page looks the way it does. These are for reviewers, not part of the product. Full evidence and sources: Docs Research.

Vs. todayWhere this lives. The path sits at the top of the Payments tab, the way Stripe's Payments sidebar opens with “Accept a payment”. That keeps the path, its guides and its tools in one sidebar, so following a link never swaps tabs. Start here links to it from the job picker.
WhyThis page covers stages 1 and 2 of the template: the job in the developer's words, then an honest fit check. The title is the job (“Accept dollar payments”), not the technology.
Vs. todayToday's /docs/payments opens with “New to how balances and ownership work on Solana? Read Core Concepts, then review Assets”, which is a concept gate in the second paragraph. That line is gone. The concepts appear inside the steps instead.
Vs. todayToday's Why Solana list is all upsides. The fit check keeps those facts but adds a comparison against cards and a Not the right fit column. MLH participants compared Solana with Stripe, so we answer that comparison in its own terms.
WhyThe guides (send, accept, subscriptions…) are unchanged and sit below the paths in the same Payments sidebar. The path is a linear 30-minute tutorial. It doesn't duplicate the guides; it links into them at the end.

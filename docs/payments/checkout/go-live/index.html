Before you take real money

Fee sponsorshipNew conceptLearn more →
By default, whoever sends a transaction needs a little SOL to pay its fee. A fee-payer service such as Kora lets your app pay instead, so customers only ever hold dollars.
Like:Absorbing the card fee instead of adding a surcharge.
Rent (storage deposit)New conceptLearn more →
Every account holds a small refundable deposit for the space it takes up. Opening a USDC account for a new customer costs about 0.002 SOL, which comes back if the account is closed.
Like:A refundable deposit on a storage locker.

Design notes

Why this page looks the way it does. These are for reviewers, not part of the product. Full evidence and sources: Docs Research.

WhyThis is stages 5 (production) and 6 (go further) of the template. The template's six stages map onto one overview page plus four steps, so each sidebar entry is a page you can finish in one sitting.
WhyFee sponsorship and rent show up here as concepts because they're production surprises. “Why does my customer need SOL?” and “why did my first transfer to a new customer cost extra?” are the two most common payments support questions.
WhyThe path ends with the next path first, then deeper docs, then human help. A dead end at the end of a path counts as a bug.
